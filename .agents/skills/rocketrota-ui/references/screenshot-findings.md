# Screenshot findings

This analysis covers the seven attachments supplied with the skill brief. Images 5 and 6 are byte-identical, so they share one analysis. The original screenshots establish visual direction. The repository defines RocketRota's brand, terminology and interaction contracts. Type sizes below are described by their visible hierarchy because screenshot scaling makes exact CSS sizes uncertain.

| Image | What creates the hierarchy | RocketRota application |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 · Mobile home and schedule | Large semibold names and screen titles give way to medium row titles and smaller grey metadata. On home, the shared edge and a repeatable gutter hold the search, paired cards and task list together. On Schedule, time headings create rhythm between low-elevation white rows on a warm neutral ground. A dark selected date, short labels, grouped avatars and labelled bottom tabs make state and navigation legible. | Use a compact date strip or day grouping where it speeds shift scanning. Give shift times and location visual priority and use a quiet tint for secondary details. Retain the existing RocketRota shell. The promotional photo, warm palette, oversized radii and floating add button would compete with operational content. |
| 2 · Desktop projects | A narrow rail and quiet toolbar leave room for the data. Large figures sit beside small explanatory labels. Dense rows use a stable grid, fine rules, aligned numeric columns, modest status pills and lighter group labels. Whitespace separates the toolbar, summary and table while each row stays compact. | Use the same alignment discipline across weekday headers, shift cells and daily totals. Group by date, zone or exception only when it helps users compare records. Use a summary metric when it drives a decision or opens the corresponding detail. The wallpaper, floating copilot and decorative metric graphics suit the reference's presentation more than a rota builder. |
| 3 · Staff detail and time off | The identity header and slim rail establish context. A stable information column and tabbed work area share a baseline. Compact date columns, light rules, a precise active underline and labelled statuses support scanning. Review controls sit beside the affected record. | Keep person, location and period visible while reviewing a timesheet. Put approved manager actions beside the affected entry and use a compact identity summary on narrow screens. Skip decorative favorites, saturated gradients and personal fields with no role in the workflow. |
| 4 · Focused sign-in | A bold greeting and a smaller explanation give way to a single aligned form. A white panel, fine borders, subtle inset inputs and soft elevation separate layers. Labels stay visible, recovery is next to the password label, and a full-width action ends the form. | Use the existing auth card and keep labels, recovery, error messages and submission in one reading path. Keep the restrained form geometry and RocketRota blue. Avoid low-contrast placeholders as instruction text, competing gradients and excessive centring on short mobile viewports. |
| 5 and 6 · Guided checkout | The stepper clearly distinguishes completed, current and future stages. Edit links line up in a prior-answers summary. The selected option has a border, tint and radio marker. An unavailable option gives a reason. A compact right-hand summary keeps the consequence visible alongside the form. | For onboarding or a genuinely multi-step review, show progress and make prior choices editable without losing context. Pair selection colour with a control marker and text. Give a blocking reason close to the action and show the scope before publishing or confirming consequential changes. On mobile, put the essential summary before commitment. Avoid adding a stepper to one-step tasks. |
| 7 · Sign-in with product context | Spacious margins frame a split composition. The left side groups logo, headline, sign-in choices and one accent action. The quiet right side uses a small product example and an undo message to show a specific benefit. Borders and a neutral wash separate the halves. | On public entry screens, an optional real RocketRota product example can clarify value. Keep the form first and usable on mobile. Avoid a rotating panel unless every slide serves a distinct purpose. An undo message works where the action is truly reversible. |

Across the set, the strongest transferable details are aligned edges, one obvious next action, subdued supporting metadata, explicit status and selection, and density tuned to the task. Use spacing between functional groups and dividers within repeated records. Preserve operational data while simplifying its presentation.
